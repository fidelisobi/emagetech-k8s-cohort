This is what separates students who get placed from students who drift
  • 📅
    Attend every session. If you miss one, watch the recording within 48 hours.
  • ✅
    Complete labs weekly. Falling behind compounds fast.
  • 🐙
    Push work to GitHub. Your commit history is your portfolio.
  • 💬
    Post in Slack weekly. Visibility and communication matter.
  • 🙋
    Ask for help early. 30 min stuck? Post in Slack immediately.
Support

    Resume Review — Every student gets a full resume review before applying. No one submits an unreviewed resume.

    Mock Interviews — Every student does at least one mock technical interview before job searching.
  • 🏆
    Placement Support — References, referral network, and alumni connections inside companies that are hiring.
