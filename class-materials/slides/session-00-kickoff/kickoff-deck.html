Support

You're Not Doing This Alone

  • 🧑‍🏫
    TA Office Hours — Tuesday 7PM + Saturday 10AM CST. Drop in with anything. No appointment, no judgment.
  • 👥
    Peer Study Groups — You'll be placed in a group of 3. One 30-min call per week. Accountability makes the difference.
  • 💬
    Slack Channel — Active 7 days a week. TAs respond within 24 hours. Post questions, wins, blockers — all of it.
  • 📄
    Resume Review — Every student gets a full resume review before applying. No one submits an unreviewed resume.
  • 🎙️
    Mock Interviews — Every student does at least one mock technical interview before job searching. Find the gaps before they cost you an offer.
  • 🏆
    Placement Support — Kenna and TAs as references. Referral network. Alumni who'll connect you inside companies.
Expectations

What We Expect From You

  • 📅
    Attend every class session. If you miss one, watch the recording within 48 hours — no exceptions.
  • ✅
    Complete the lab in the same week as the session. Falling behind compounds fast. Stay current.
  • 📢
    Post in Slack every week. Monday check-ins are non-negotiable. Visibility matters — for your learning and ours.
  • 🐙
    Push your work to GitHub weekly. Your commit history is your portfolio. Make it green.
  • 🙋
    Ask for help before you fall behind. 30-minute rule. Always.
The honest truth: The students who get jobs aren't the smartest in the room. They're the ones who show up every week, push through the hard parts, and ask for help when they need it.
