## 🎯 The Goal

By the time you finish this cohort, you should be able to walk into any interview and speak confidently about:

- What you built (15 projects — real artifacts, not tutorials)
- How you solved real problems (runbooks, security hardening, observability)
- Why you made architectural decisions (GitOps vs manual apply, Sealed Secrets vs Vault, etc.)
- What you'd do differently next time (shows maturity and self-reflection)

That narrative is your competitive advantage. This folder helps you tell it.
