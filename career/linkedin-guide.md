# 🔗 LinkedIn Optimization Guide

## Why LinkedIn Matters for K8s Roles

Most K8s jobs are filled through recruiter outreach or referrals — not cold applications.
A strong LinkedIn profile means recruiters find YOU.

---

## Headline Formula

Don't use your job title. Use keywords recruiters search for.

**Weak:**
> Student | Looking for opportunities

**Strong:**
> Kubernetes Engineer | GitOps | Cloud Native | GKE · EKS · AKS | CKA Candidate

**Why this works:** Recruiters search for "Kubernetes Engineer" or "GKE" — your headline must match.

---

## About Section (250–300 words)

Formula:
1. What you do (technically)
2. What you've built (reference the cohort projects)
3. What you're looking for
4. Call to action

Example:
> I'm a Kubernetes engineer focused on GitOps, platform engineering, and cloud-native security. Over the past several months I've completed an intensive hands-on Kubernetes cohort where I built 15 production-grade projects — from containerizing multi-tier applications with raw YAML to architecting a fully automated GitOps platform with ArgoCD, Sealed Secrets, Prometheus, and Kyverno.
>
> Key things I've built: a full observability stack (Prometheus + Grafana + Loki + AlertManager), a GitOps pipeline that deploys via PR merge, a Kyverno policy library enforcing security baselines across namespaces, and a security hardening exercise that pushed a cluster from 35% to 92% NeuVector compliance score.
>
> I'm working toward CKA certification and looking for Kubernetes/platform engineering roles where I can contribute from day one.
>
> Open to opportunities — feel free to connect or reach out directly.

## Posting Strategy

Post 1–2 times per week while job hunting. Ideas:
- "I just finished Project 8 — here's what building an observability stack from scratch taught me about Prometheus vs Loki..."
- Share an interesting thing you debugged ("Spent 2 hours on a CrashLoopBackOff. Turned out the liveness probe path was wrong. Here's the checklist I now use...")
- Comment thoughtfully on Kubernetes posts by people like Kelsey Hightower, Liz Rice, or the CNCF account

Recruiters look at activity. An active profile signals an active practitioner.

---

## Networking Moves

- Connect with everyone in your cohort — their networks become your network
- Connect with every TA and instructor
- Join LinkedIn groups: "Kubernetes", "Cloud Native Computing", "DevOps Engineers"
- Follow: CNCF, Kelsey Hightower, Liz Rice (Aqua Security), Brendan Burns (K8s co-creator)
- Message recruiters at companies you want to work at — direct, brief, specific is best:

> "Hi [Name], I'm a Kubernetes engineer finishing a hands-on cohort where I built a full GitOps + observability platform. I noticed [Company] is hiring for a K8s role — happy to share my GitHub if it's helpful. Either way, open to connecting."
