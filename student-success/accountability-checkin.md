# ✅ Weekly Accountability Check-In

Every Monday, post your check-in in the `#kubernetes-january-2026-cohort` Slack channel.

It takes 3 minutes. It keeps you honest. It keeps TAs informed. It builds the habit of communicating your work — which is exactly what you'll do in your first engineering job.

---

## The Template

Copy and paste this every Monday:

```
📊 Week [X] Check-In — [Your Name]

✅ Completed last week:
- [Lab/Project/Topic you finished]

🔨 Working on this week:
- [Lab/Project/Topic you're doing now]

❓ Stuck on / need help with:
- [Specific question or blocker — or "nothing, I'm good"]

📈 Morale check:
🟢 Feeling good  /  🟡 Hanging in there  /  🔴 Need help
```

---

## Example Check-In

```
📊 Week 5 Check-In — Idris

✅ Completed last week:
- Lab 14 (Pod Lifecycle) — liveness probes finally clicked
- Started Project 2 YAML library — got Deployment and StatefulSet templates done

🔨 Working on this week:
- Finishing Project 2 (DaemonSet, Job, CronJob templates)
- Lab 15 (ConfigMaps and Secrets)

❓ Stuck on / need help with:
- CronJob syntax — my schedule expression isn't firing, will bring to office hours Tuesday

📈 Morale check:
🟡 Hanging in there — had a rough week but getting back on track
```

---

## Why This Works

- **TAs can see who needs help** before they fall too far behind
- **You build the habit** of communicating your progress (expected in every engineering role)
- **Classmates can pair up** — if you're stuck on Lab 14 and someone just finished it, they can help
- **It keeps you honest** — publicly committing to a weekly goal makes you more likely to do it

---

## For TAs — How to Use Check-Ins

Every Monday, scan the check-ins and:

1. **🔴 Red morale** → DM the student within 24 hours. Don't wait.
2. **Missing check-in** → DM the student: *"Hey, didn't see your check-in this week — everything ok?"*
3. **Same blocker 2 weeks in a row** → Escalate to Kenna.
4. **Student ahead of pace** → Ask them to buddy up with someone behind.

Respond to every check-in with at least a 👍 reaction. It takes 1 second and signals "I see you."
