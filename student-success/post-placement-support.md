# 🔄 Post-Placement Support

Getting the job is not the finish line. The first 90 days determine whether a student thrives or struggles.

This document covers what we do after placement — for both the student and the admin team.

---

## For Students — Your First 90 Days

### Before Day 1

- [ ] Connect with your new team on LinkedIn
- [ ] Review the company's tech stack (GitHub org if public, blog posts, job descriptions)
- [ ] Set up your dev environment based on what you know about their setup
- [ ] Complete Project 16 (Terraform) if you haven't — you'll likely be provisioning infrastructure early
- [ ] Review your Project 15 capstone — you'll reference it in your first 1:1s

### Day 1–30: Listen and Learn

Your job in the first month is NOT to fix everything. It's to understand how things work.

- **Observe before suggesting.** There are reasons things are the way they are.
- **Ask questions constantly.** Write them down, bring them to 1:1s.
- **Build a glossary.** Every company has internal names for things. Document them.
- **Find your go-to person.** Identify who to ask when you're stuck. Build that relationship early.

Questions to ask in your first week:
- *"What does the deployment process look like end to end?"*
- *"What's the on-call rotation and what does a typical incident look like?"*
- *"What's the biggest pain point on the platform right now?"*
- *"What does success look like for this role in 6 months?"*

### Day 30–60: Contribute

- Start taking ownership of small, well-defined tasks
- Make your first PR that goes to production
- Document something that wasn't documented before (instant goodwill with the team)
- Propose one small improvement — with context, trade-offs, and a plan

### Day 60–90: Build Credibility

- You should own at least one thing — a service, a runbook, a dashboard
- You should be the person others come to for at least one topic
- Start the conversation about what you'll own in Q2/Q3
- If you haven't started CKA prep — start now

## For Admins — The Check-In Schedule

### 30-Day Check-In
DM every placed student at 30 days:

```
Hey [Name]! It's been a month since you started at [Company] — how's it going?

Quick questions:
1. What's the biggest thing you've learned so far?
2. Anything from the cohort you wish we had covered more?
3. Is there anything you're struggling with we can help with?

So proud of you 🚀
```

### 60-Day Check-In
```
Hey [Name]! Two months in — how are you settling?

1. What are you working on right now?
2. Any gaps in your knowledge you're feeling?
3. Would you be open to doing a mock interview with a newer cohort student?
   (You'd be AMAZING at this — and it reinforces your own learning)
```

### 90-Day Check-In
```
Hey [Name]! 3 months — you made it through the hardest stretch.

1. What's one thing you're proud of from your first 90 days?
2. Are you being recognized / promoted / given more responsibility?
3. Are you happy? (Real question — your wellbeing matters)
4. Would you share your story for the next cohort's kickoff?
```

## CKA Certification Support

We encourage every placed student to pursue CKA within 6 months.

**Why it matters:**
- Formal validation of skills you already have
- Required or preferred for many senior roles
- Salary increase of $15k–$25k at some companies

**Resources:**
- [Killer.sh](https://killer.sh) — best CKA practice simulator (comes free with the exam voucher)
- [KodeKloud CKA Course](https://kodekloud.com/courses/certified-kubernetes-administrator-cka/) — structured study
- [Certified Kubernetes Administrator](https://training.linuxfoundation.org/certification/certified-kubernetes-administrator-cka/)

**Cohort support:**
- Post your CKA study goal in Slack — accountability helps
- We'll organize a CKA study group for anyone preparing at the same time
- Ask alumni who've passed for their tips

## Alumni Mentorship

Placed students who are willing: spend 30 minutes per month with a current cohort student.

**What you do:**
- One call per month (30 min, no prep required)
- Answer questions about the job search, day-to-day work, how to grow
- Share what you wish you'd known

**What you get:**
- You consolidate your own knowledge by explaining it
- You build a mentor reputation (visible on LinkedIn)
- You're the person who helped someone else get placed
- Karma is real in the tech community

To opt in: post in Slack or DM Kenna.

