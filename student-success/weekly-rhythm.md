# 📅 Weekly Rhythm

Structure is the difference between students who finish and students who drift.
Follow this schedule every week — no exceptions.

---

## The Weekly Schedule

```
MONDAY
  ├── Post your weekly check-in in Slack (see accountability-checkin.md)
  ├── Review: what lab or project are you completing this week?
  └── Unblock yourself: post any questions from last week NOW

TUESDAY
  ├── TA Office Hours — 7:00 PM CST (drop-in, no appointment)
  ├── If stuck on anything: this is your chance
  └── Pair with a classmate on the current lab

WEDNESDAY
  ├── CLASS SESSION — mandatory
  ├── Take notes, ask questions, follow along in your cluster
  └── After class: spend 30 min reviewing what was covered

THURSDAY
  ├── Work on the week's lab
  ├── Post progress in Slack (even "I'm 50% through lab 14")
  └── Help a classmate if you're ahead

FRIDAY
  ├── Complete the week's lab ← this is your weekly milestone
  ├── Push your work to your GitHub fork
  └── Post completion in Slack: "✅ Lab X done"

SATURDAY
  ├── Optional: TA Office Hours — 10:00 AM CST
  ├── Peer study group (30 min with your trio)
  └── Get ahead: start the next lab or work on your current project

SUNDAY
  ├── Project work day — no class, no pressure, just build
  ├── Optional: share what you built in #project-showcase
  └── Prep for Monday check-in: what did you accomplish this week?
```

## Non-Negotiables

These are the habits that separate students who get jobs from students who don't:

1. **Post in Slack every week** — even one sentence. Visibility matters.
2. **Never be stuck for more than 30 minutes without asking** — post in Slack, go to office hours.
3. **Push your work to GitHub every week** — your commit history is part of your portfolio.
4. **Attend every class session** — if you miss one, watch the recording within 48 hours.
5. **Complete the lab in the same week** — falling behind compounds fast.

---

## If You Fall Behind

Life happens. Here's how to catch up:

- **1 week behind:** Watch the recording, do the lab on the weekend, post your catch-up in Slack.
- **2 weeks behind:** DM a TA immediately. Get on a catch-up plan.
- **3+ weeks behind:** Schedule a 1:1 with Kenna. We will find a path forward.

No one has ever caught up by quietly hoping it gets better. Reach out early.
