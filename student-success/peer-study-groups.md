# 👥 Peer Study Groups

You were assigned to a study group of 3 students at the start of the cohort.
This group is your support system for the next 6 months.

---

## Your Commitment

**One 30-minute call per week.** That's it.

No agenda. No slides. Just three people checking in on each other.

---

## The 30-Minute Format

```
0:00 — 0:05  Round robin: what did you do this week?
0:05 — 0:20  Someone shares their screen — work through a blocker together
0:20 — 0:28  What is each person doing before next week?
0:28 — 0:30  Any shoutouts / wins to share in Slack?
```

If nobody has a blocker, spend the 15 minutes doing a lab together. Two people working through the same problem learn faster than one person alone.

## Group Norms

Set these expectations with your group on your first call:

1. **Show up.** If you can't make it, give 24 hours notice.
2. **Be honest about where you are.** No one benefits from pretending they understand.
3. **Help each other, don't do each other's work.** Walk someone through a concept — don't just give them the answer.
4. **Celebrate each other's wins.** When someone finishes a project, say so in the main channel.

---

## What to Do When a Group Member Goes Quiet

If someone misses 2 calls in a row:
1. DM them: *"Hey, haven't seen you — just checking in. Everything ok?"*
2. If no response after 24 hours, tag a TA in Slack.

Don't let a classmate disappear quietly. One message might be the thing that keeps them in the cohort.
