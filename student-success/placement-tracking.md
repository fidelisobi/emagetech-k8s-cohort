# 📊 Placement Tracking (Admin)

This document is for TAs and Kenna to track student job placements.
Update it when a student lands a role.

---

## How to Record a Placement

When a student posts that they got a job:
1. Add them to the table below
2. DM them within 24 hours: *"Huge congratulations! Can you share: company name, role title, and if you're comfortable — salary range? We track this to help future cohorts."*
3. Post their story in `#kubernetes-january-2026-cohort` (with their permission)
4. Add them to the Alumni mentorship pool

## The Placement Announcement Template

Post this in the Slack channel when a student gets placed (with their permission):

```
🎉 WE HAVE A PLACEMENT!

Huge congratulations to [Name] (@slack-handle) who just accepted an offer 
as a [Role Title] at [Company]!

[Name] joined the cohort with [brief background — e.g., "a networking background 
and zero Kubernetes experience"]. Over the past [X] months, they:

✅ Completed all 20 class labs
✅ Built [highlight 2-3 projects]
✅ [Any notable achievement — e.g., "pushed their cluster security posture from 35% to 91%"]

This is what the work looks like. This is what's possible.

If you're still in the job search — stay the course. Your moment is coming.

Congratulations [Name] 🚀
```

This post is the single most powerful motivation tool in the cohort. Never skip it.
