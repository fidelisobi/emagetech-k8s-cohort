# 🔍 Job Search Guide

You finished the cohort. Your resume is reviewed. Your mock interview is done.
Now it's time to find the job. Here's the system.

---

## The Three Channels That Work

### Channel 1 — Recruiter Outreach (Highest Success Rate)
Recruiters actively search LinkedIn for Kubernetes keywords. If your profile is optimized (`career/linkedin-guide.md`), they come to you. When they reach out — respond within 24 hours, always.

**What to say when a recruiter reaches out:**
> "Thanks for reaching out. I'm a Kubernetes engineer with hands-on experience in GitOps, Helm, observability, and security hardening — I recently completed a full-stack capstone platform project. I'm open to [remote / hybrid / your preference]. Happy to share my GitHub and schedule a call."

### Channel 2 — Referrals (Fastest Path to Interview)
One referral skips the ATS filter and puts you directly in front of a hiring manager. Your cohort classmates, TAs, and Kenna are your network. Use it.

**How to ask for a referral:**
> "Hey [Name], I saw [Company] is hiring for a [Role]. You're connected there — any chance you'd be willing to refer me or share my resume? Happy to return the favor anytime."

That's it. One sentence. Most people say yes.

### Channel 3 — Direct Applications (Necessary, but Slow)
Apply to 5–10 roles per week minimum. Use a tracker (see below). Customize the first 2 lines of your cover note for each role.

## Where to Find K8s Roles

| Job Board | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| [CNCF Job Board](https://jobs.cncf.io) | Best quality — cloud-native specific roles |
| [LinkedIn Jobs](https://linkedin.com/jobs) | Volume + recruiter messages |
| [Wellfound / AngelList](https://wellfound.com) | Startup roles, often remote-first |
| [Dice](https://dice.com) | Strong for US tech / contract roles |
| [Y Combinator Jobs](https://ycombinator.com/jobs) | High-quality startups |
| [Greenhouse](https://greenhouse.io) | Many mid-size tech companies |

**Search terms that convert:**
- `Kubernetes Engineer`
- `Platform Engineer`
- `Site Reliability Engineer` (SRE)
- `DevOps Engineer Kubernetes`
- `Cloud Native Engineer`
- `Infrastructure Engineer GKE`
- `K8s GitOps`

## The Recruiter Outreach Template

Send this to 5–10 recruiters per week on LinkedIn:

```
Hi [Recruiter Name],

I'm a Kubernetes engineer who recently completed an intensive hands-on cohort — 
built a full GitOps + observability + security platform using ArgoCD, Prometheus, 
Kyverno, and NeuVector. GitHub: [your link]

I'm exploring [Platform Engineer / SRE / DevOps] roles — ideally [remote / 
hybrid / your preference]. Would love to connect if you're working on anything 
relevant.

[Your name]
```

Short. Specific. Has a GitHub link. That's all a recruiter needs.

---

## Following Up

**After applying:** Wait 7 days. If no response, send one follow-up:
> "Hi [name], I applied for the [role] on [date] and wanted to reiterate my strong interest. I have hands-on experience with [2 specific things from the job description]. Happy to share anything else. Thanks."

**After a screen:** Send a thank-you within 2 hours:
> "Thanks for the conversation today. I'm excited about the [role] — especially [specific thing they mentioned]. Looking forward to next steps."

**After a technical interview:** Same format. Reference something specific.

---

## When You're Not Hearing Back

If you're sending applications and hearing nothing — the problem is usually one of:

1. **Resume not passing ATS** — add more keywords from job descriptions
2. **Not applying to enough roles** — 5/week minimum, aim for 10
3. **Targeting too-senior roles** — 3+ years experience required means they mean it
4. **GitHub is empty** — this is a dealbreaker for K8s roles. Pin your best repos.

If you're getting screens but not getting to technical rounds — the problem is:
1. **Resume overstates experience** — calibrate expectations
2. **Recruiter screen answer about salary** — research market rates first

If you're getting technical rounds but not getting offers — do another mock interview.
