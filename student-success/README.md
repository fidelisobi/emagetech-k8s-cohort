## 🏆 The North Star

> **Every student who finishes this cohort should have a job within 90 days.**

That is the goal. Everything in this directory exists to close the gap between "completed the class" and "accepted an offer."

## 🆘 The Drop-Off Points

Know where students struggle and intervene early:

**Week 2–3:** YAML + CLI overwhelm. Fix: pair them with a classmate, push them to office hours.

**End of class:** Structure disappears. Fix: weekly check-ins, project deadlines, peer groups.

**Job search week 3+:** No responses, losing motivation. Fix: resume review, mock interview, recruiter outreach template.

When you see a student going quiet in Slack — DM them within 24 hours. Don't wait.
